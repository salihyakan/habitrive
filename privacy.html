Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Habitrive, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: Name and email address (via Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, or email/password registration)
  • App data: Habits, goals, streaks, and progress data you enter in the app
  • Device information: Device type and operating system version for analytics
  • Purchase information: Subscription status managed via RevenueCat (we do not store payment details)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Habitrive app experience
  • To sync your data across devices via Firebase
  • To send habit reminders and notifications (only if you grant permission)
  • To generate personalized AI coaching and weekly reports (Pro feature)
  • To process and manage your subscription

3. Third-Party Services

Habitrive uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, and analytics — Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management — Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API: AI-powered coaching features — Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Firebase servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. We do not sell your data to third parties.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app's Profile settings. Upon deletion, your data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Habitrive is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us.
